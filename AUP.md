# Infiltr — Acceptable Use Policy

Infiltr runs active security tools (port scanners, vulnerability scanners,
credential brute-forcers, exploit-framework modules) against targets you specify.
Running these tools against systems without authorization is illegal in most
jurisdictions (e.g. US CFAA, UK Computer Misuse Act) and violates this policy.

By creating an account and by launching each scan you represent and warrant that:

1. **Authorization.** You own the target, or you have explicit written permission
   from the owner to perform security testing against it. You will produce that
   authorization on request.
2. **Scope.** You will only scan targets within the authorized scope. You will not
   use Infiltr to scan third parties, shared infrastructure, or systems you do not
   control without permission.
3. **No unlawful use.** You will not use Infiltr to gain unauthorized access,
   disrupt service, exfiltrate data, or for any unlawful purpose.
4. **Data.** Scan output may capture sensitive data (credentials, PII). You are
   responsible for handling it lawfully. The operator may retain scan data for a
   limited period for operation and incident response.

**Enforcement.** Every scan is logged with the requesting user, target, source IP,
and your per-scan authorization attestation. The operator may suspend or delete
accounts and refuse or terminate scans that violate this policy, and may report
unlawful activity to authorities.

Per-scan attestation (`authorization_attestation`) and acceptance of this policy
at registration (`accepted_tos`) are required. Providing a false attestation is a
violation of this policy and may be a criminal offense.
